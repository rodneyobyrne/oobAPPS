# Claims and Evidence Governance

The oobAPPS experience may explain oobCREATIVE beliefs, capabilities and service paths, but it must not manufacture proof.

## Claim classes

### Brand belief

A statement about what oobCREATIVE believes or values.

Example type: communication and media should be used responsibly.

### Capability claim

A statement about work oobCREATIVE can perform.

Requires a real skill, defined delivery capability or supported service scope.

### Experience claim

A statement that Rodney or oobCREATIVE has performed work in a particular context.

Requires verified source evidence.

### Observable outcome

A documented change that occurred during or after work.

Requires evidence that the change actually occurred.

### Measurable result

A numeric outcome such as traffic, applications, engagement, cost, time, conversion, retention or revenue.

Requires:

- source;
- timeframe;
- baseline;
- measurement definition;
- attribution limits.

### Testimonial

A client or collaborator statement.

Requires approved wording and permission/attribution.

## Rules

Do not:

- convert a desired service outcome into a past result;
- infer a metric from an anecdote;
- strengthen a claim by removing limitations;
- imply sole causation when other factors contributed;
- use a client name publicly without permission;
- invent a quote that sounds like a testimonial;
- treat a proposal, job description or draft as proof that work was completed.

## Current proof status

The private oobCREATIVE source system does not yet contain a complete verified proof register.

Until a deliberate public proof package is added to oobAPPS:

- use restrained capability language;
- avoid specific performance claims;
- do not generate case studies from memory;
- ask for or retrieve supporting evidence before using a project as proof.

## App result claims

Assessment or module outputs should distinguish:

- direct user input;
- deterministic result;
- model interpretation;
- hypothesis needing confirmation;
- benchmark or external evidence, if any.

Do not describe a directional assessment as a validated diagnostic or industry benchmark unless that status is actually supported.

## Product economics claims

Internal launch sensitivity tables are planning math, not expected conversion rates or revenue forecasts.

Do not publish them as evidence of product demand.
