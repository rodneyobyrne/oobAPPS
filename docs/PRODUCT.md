# Product Definition

## Working product thesis

oobAPPS turns selected oobCREATIVE communication strategy, decision logic and human-context methods into conversational tools that can do useful top-of-funnel work before expensive human attention is warranted.

The product is not a generic chatbot and is not an AI replacement for oobCREATIVE services.

Its role is closer to adaptive communications infrastructure:

**recognize → clarify → provide value → preserve useful context by choice → route appropriately**

## Economic job

Traditional collateral requires strategy, writing, design, production and distribution before a specific recipient expresses intent.

Conversational collateral can respond after a person has already expressed some intent in conversation. This allows communication effort to increase with demonstrated need rather than paying the full production cost in advance for every possible audience scenario.

The app should absorb work historically performed by:

- introductory flyers and brochures
- FAQs and educational handouts
- basic lead magnets
- preliminary questionnaires
- orientation calls
- simple diagnostic worksheets
- introductory recommendations
- reusable sales-support collateral

It should not pretend those functions are equivalent to full strategy, creative development, consulting or implementation.

## Appropriate-fidelity ladder

| Level | Purpose | Typical output |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Recognition | Establish whether the situation is relevant | useful distinction, short answer, route |
| 2. Orientation | Help the user understand the problem space | explanation, options, boundary |
| 3. Structured result | Turn user input into a useful bounded result | assessment, workfile, decision |
| 4. Automated paid product | Produce a deeper reusable asset without human delivery | implementation pack, structured toolkit |
| 5. Human review | Apply professional judgment to ambiguity or consequence | review, consultation, recommendation |
| 6. Implementation | Create, configure, design or coordinate the solution | oobCREATIVE project |
| 7. Ongoing support | Maintain or improve the system over time | recurring support |

The ladder governs scope, not truthfulness. Accuracy, privacy and evidence standards do not decline at earlier levels.

## Core audience condition

The product is best aligned with capable people whose responsibilities, ideas or ambitions have outgrown the communication or operating structures supporting them.

The useful commercial signal is not that someone is "behind." It is that a communication or execution gap has a visible cost such as leadership attention, repeated revisions, staff overload, lost opportunity, fragmented customer experience or underused expertise.

## Recognition model

Keep personas as creative and strategic compression tools, but do not classify public users by persona name.

The app should reason with:

1. **Situation** — what is happening now
2. **Trigger** — why it matters now
3. **Responsibility** — what the user is accountable for
4. **Protected value** — what change must not damage
5. **Current workaround** — how the problem is being absorbed today
6. **Cost/consequence** — what the current pattern is consuming or risking
7. **Competing priorities** — what can both be true at once
8. **Desired movement** — what useful improvement looks like
9. **Evidence** — what is supported
10. **Unknowns** — what should not be assumed
11. **Next decision** — the smallest useful action

The system should recognize expressed patterns in work context. It should not infer hidden emotional or psychological traits and present them as fact.

## MVP

The first release should use three existing oobSKILLS capabilities because they demonstrate different product strengths:

### Customer Flow Health

Demonstrates explainable operational recognition and the ability to conclude that a technology project is not justified when the evidence does not support one.

### AI Workday Review

Demonstrates the human/technology bridge by beginning with a responsibility rather than an AI product and keeping ownership, review and stopping conditions visible.

### Audience Review

Demonstrates communications recognition: what customers need to understand, what creates trust, and what message work may help the right person decide.

## Conversation-first routing

The user should not be required to choose one of those modules at the beginning.

A conversational router should:

1. receive the user's stated problem or question;
2. identify one or more plausible situation patterns;
3. ask a minimum clarifying question when needed;
4. confirm rather than assume;
5. invoke the smallest useful module;
6. explain the result in context;
7. offer a relevant next step only after value has been delivered.

## Saved relationship

Anonymous use should remain possible for the free core experience where technically practical.

A saved relationship begins when the user deliberately chooses continuity, such as saving a result, receiving a workfile, purchasing a product or asking for human help.

Saved relationship context should be useful to the user, not merely useful to marketing.

## Revenue paths

The MVP should support three commercial outcomes without requiring all three to succeed immediately:

1. **Automated product revenue** — a bounded digital product based on demonstrated need
2. **Qualified service revenue** — a strong-fit human oobCREATIVE project or review
3. **Reduced acquisition cost** — less human time and speculative collateral production before qualification

Do not build a large digital-product catalog before real usage identifies which paid next step people actually want.

## Success hypothesis

The launch succeeds if oobAPPS can engage a stranger without founder involvement, recognize enough of the situation to provide legitimate value, preserve trust, create an optional continuing relationship, and route some users into relevant automated products or human services.
