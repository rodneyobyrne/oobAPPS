# Initial Situation Seeds

These situation records are public-safe routing seeds derived from selected oobCREATIVE audience strategy.

They are not diagnoses, personas, industries or mandatory products.

Their job is to help the app recognize a plausible kind of problem and ask a useful confirming question.

---

## 1. Too much still depends on one person

### Recognizable situation

Important decisions, approvals, customer context or quality control repeatedly return to one person even after other capable people are involved.

### Possible protected value

Quality, consistency, customer trust or professional judgment.

Do not assume which value applies. Confirm it.

### Common structural signals

- routine work stops for approval;
- staff repeatedly ask for context;
- founder/leader rewrites or corrects finished work;
- knowledge is available mostly through one person;
- delegation exists but judgment has not become transferable.

### Useful confirmation question

Is the bigger issue that other people do not yet have enough context to make the decision, or that releasing review would risk a drop in quality?

### Possible next modules

- Founder Bottleneck Review (future)
- Audience Review
- Workflow & Systems Review (future)

### Likely service families

- Communication Systems and Practical AI
- Message, Positioning and Identity
- Ongoing Communications Support

## 2. Valuable work is difficult to explain

### Recognizable situation

The organization or expert knows the work has value, but explanations become too long, too technical, inconsistent or oversimplified for the people who need to decide.

### Possible protected value

Meaning, mission, intellectual depth, credibility or accuracy.

### Common structural signals

- repeated rewriting for different audiences;
- the same program/service described differently by different people;
- fear that simplifying the message will flatten the work;
- visitors understand the category but not the difference/value;
- internal language and customer language do not match.

### Useful confirmation question

Are you mainly trying to make the work easier to understand, or are you trying to make sure simplifying it does not strip away what makes it valuable?

### Possible next modules

- Audience Review
- Website Message Clarity Review (future)

### Likely service families

- Message, Positioning and Identity
- Website Strategy and Visual Refresh
- Campaigns, Launches and Storytelling

## 3. Communication has become structurally chaotic

### Recognizable situation

Communication work is driven by incoming requests, urgent revisions, unclear ownership or repeated starting from zero.

### Possible protected value

Professional agency, communication quality, staff capacity or consistency.

### Common structural signals

- every request feels urgent;
- approvals are inconsistent;
- teams recreate similar content repeatedly;
- requests arrive without enough context;
- communication depends on informal memory or chat threads.

### Useful confirmation question

Is the bigger problem the amount of communication work, or the way requests and approvals move through the team?

- Workflow & Systems Review (future)
- AI Workday Review
- Audience Review

## 4. Customers do not understand the value of expertise

### Recognizable situation

An expert, professional or specialized business has real capability, but prospective customers do not quickly understand what the expertise changes for them.

### Possible protected value

Intellectual depth, credibility, professional identity or appropriate positioning.

### Common structural signals

- service descriptions sound generic;
- the user explains better in conversation than on the website;
- prospects compare the work on price/category alone;
- the offer contains nuance that is difficult to package;
- the expert resists language that feels simplistic or sales-heavy.

### Useful confirmation question

When people misunderstand the value, are they missing what you actually do, or are they missing why the difference matters to them?

### Possible next modules

- Audience Review
- Website Message Clarity Review (future)

### Likely service families

- Message, Positioning and Identity
- Website Strategy and Visual Refresh

## 5. The organization wants a practical, responsible use of AI

### Recognizable situation

Someone sees possible value in AI but does not want a tool-first implementation, uncontrolled staff use or automation that weakens responsibility and trust.

### Possible protected value

Accuracy, responsibility, trust, privacy, customer experience or professional judgment.

### Common structural signals

- staff are already experimenting inconsistently;
- leadership has an AI mandate without a defined use case;
- the organization is choosing tools before defining responsibility;
- sensitive or consequential work needs a review boundary;
- user wants relief from repetitive work without replacing human ownership.

### Useful confirmation question

What recurring responsibility are you actually hoping AI could make easier?

### Possible next modules

- AI Workday Review
- AI Fit Check (future)

### Likely service families

- Communication Systems and Practical AI

## 6. A project has stalled, failed or become hard to trust

### Recognizable situation

A website, automation, vendor engagement or digital project has gone wrong, and the next decision is affected by uncertainty about what can still be trusted or reused.

### Possible protected value

Decision safety, budget, trust, control or the usable work already invested.

### Common structural signals

- user wants to restart everything;
- vendor handoff is incomplete;
- no one can explain what currently works;
- technical debt and strategic failure are being mixed together;
- prior overpromising makes the user cautious about the next recommendation.

### Useful confirmation question

Before deciding what to replace, do you know which parts are actually broken and which parts may still be usable?

### Possible next modules

- Digital Project Recovery Review (future)
- Workflow & Systems Review (future)

### Likely service families

- Website Strategy and Visual Refresh

## 7. An idea is ready for an honest test

### Recognizable situation

The person has developed an idea enough that more private refinement may be less valuable than a bounded encounter with real users, customers or stakeholders.

### Possible protected value

Possibility, credibility, identity, budget or freedom to change direction.

### Common structural signals

- planning has outpaced evidence;
- user is considering a large build before testing demand;
- offer language is still changing;
- the person wants confidence before becoming more public;
- the idea could be tested through a much smaller interaction.

### Useful confirmation question

What is the smallest real-world response that would tell you whether this direction deserves more investment?

### Possible next modules

- Idea-to-Test Review (future)
- Audience Review

### Likely service families

- Campaigns, Launches and Storytelling
- Message, Positioning and Identity

## 8. Growth is making customer flow harder to manage

### Recognizable situation

Customer information, follow-up or everyday coordination has become harder as volume, staff, tools or service complexity increased.

### Possible protected value

Customer experience, staff capacity, responsiveness, continuity or existing tools that still work well.

### Common structural signals

- repeated data entry;
- missed calls or unclear follow-up ownership;
- customer history spread across several places;
- staff manually bridge systems;
- growth creates more administrative work rather than proportional capacity;
- user assumes the answer is a new platform before the handoff is understood.

### Useful confirmation question

Is the biggest pain currently missed customer follow-up, finding the right customer context, or the amount of manual work between systems?

### Possible next modules

- Customer Flow Health Review
- Workflow & Systems Review (future)
- Customer Contact Workflow Review (future)

### Likely service families

- Communication Systems and Practical AI

# Routing rule

A situation seed should only narrow the next question or module choice.

It must not be used to assert that a person has a particular personality, emotional condition or internal persona classification.

When the user's description contradicts the seed, the user's description wins.
